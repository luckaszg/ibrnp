---
title: What we believe
description: A summary of our faith, according to the 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith.
---

What we believe is set out in full in the [Second London Baptist Confession of Faith of 1689](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/). Below is a short summary with links to the relevant chapters.

## The Holy Scriptures

The Bible is the Word of God, inspired, infallible and sufficient; it is the only rule of faith and obedience. [Chapter 1](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/1/)

## God

There is one only living and true God, who subsists eternally in three Persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. He has decreed all that comes to pass and governs all things by His providence. [Chapters 2–5](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/2/)

## Man and sin

God created man in His image, upright and good; but through Adam's fall all mankind became dead in sin and under condemnation. [Chapter 6](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/6/)

## The covenant of grace and Christ the Mediator

God was pleased to save His people through a covenant of grace, revealed progressively from the promise to Adam and fully established in the **new covenant** in Christ. Jesus Christ, true God and true man, is the only Mediator: Prophet, Priest and King. [Chapters 7–8](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/7/)

## Salvation

Salvation is entirely of grace. God effectually calls His elect, justifies them by faith alone on the ground of Christ's righteousness, adopts them, sanctifies them and keeps them to the end. [Chapters 10–18](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/10/)

## God's law and the Christian life

The moral law, summarised in the Ten Commandments, remains a rule of life for the believer — not as a covenant of works, but as the expression of God's will. [Chapters 19–21](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/19/)

## Worship and the Lord's Day

God may be worshipped only in the way He has instituted. The first day of the week is the Lord's Day, the Christian Sabbath. [Chapter 22](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/22/)

## The Church

Christ is the only Head of the Church. Each local church is made up of baptized believers, governed by elders and served by deacons. [Chapters 26–27](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/26/)

## Baptism and the Lord's Supper

Baptism, by immersion, belongs to those who profess faith in Christ. The Lord's Supper is a memorial of His death and a means of grace to His people. [Chapters 28–30](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/28/)

## The last things

Christ will return in glory to judge the living and the dead. The righteous will enter eternal life and the wicked eternal punishment. [Chapters 31–32](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/31/)

---

## Distinctives

**Baptist covenant theology (1689 Federalism).** We understand the new covenant to be the covenant of grace fully revealed, and its members to be those regenerated by the Spirit. Hence our name: *Nuevo Pacto* — New Covenant.

**The doctrines of grace.** God saves sovereignly: He elects unconditionally, Christ redeems His people, the Spirit effectually regenerates, and God preserves His own to the end.

**The regulative principle of worship.** Public worship is limited to what God has commanded in His Word.

**Believer's baptism.** Baptism is the sign of the new covenant for those who profess faith in Christ.
