title: Our worship
description: What to expect when you visit on the Lord's Day.
---

We gather every Lord's Day to worship God in the way He has appointed in His Word. We believe God regulates how He is to be worshipped (*the regulative principle*), so our worship is simple and centred on Scripture.

> God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.
> <cite>John 4:24</cite>

## Order of service

1. Catechism reading
2. Invocation
3. Hymns
4. Scripture reading
5. Expository sermon

## Preaching

Preaching is at the heart of the service. We preach expositionally, working through books of the Bible, so that the congregation hears the whole counsel of God.

## Sunday school

Before the service, at 9:00, we meet for Sunday school.

## The ordinances

We celebrate the **Lord's Supper** twice a month. **Baptism** is administered by immersion to those who profess faith in Christ.

## Children

Children stay with their families during the service; we believe it is good for them to hear the Word together with their parents. There is a nursery and a nursing room.

## Practical details

The service lasts about an hour and three quarters. Access is easy: the building is on a closed street. There is no on-site parking.
