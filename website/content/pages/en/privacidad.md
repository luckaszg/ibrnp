---
title: Privacy policy
description: How we handle your data on this site.
---

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

**Contact form.** If you write to us, we use your name, email and message only to reply. The message is delivered by an email-sending service and is never published or shared with third parties.

**Videos and maps.** YouTube videos and the map load only when you press the button to play or show them. At that point YouTube or OpenStreetMap may receive technical data from your browser.

**Reading preferences.** Text size, theme and your last reading position are stored only in your browser.

For any questions, write to us at the address on [Visit us](/en/visit/).
