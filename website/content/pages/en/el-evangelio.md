---
title: The Gospel
description: The good news of salvation by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.
---

The word *gospel* means "good news". It is the announcement of what God has done to save sinners through His Son, Jesus Christ.

## God is holy

God made us for His glory. He is our Creator and our Judge, perfectly holy and just, and we must give account to Him for our lives.

> Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.
> <cite>Isaiah 6:3</cite>

## We are sinners

We have all broken God's law in thought, word and deed. Sin is not merely a mistake: it is rebellion against God, and it deserves His just judgment. No one can save himself by his own good works.

> For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.
> <cite>Romans 3:23</cite>

## Christ is the Saviour

In His great love, God sent His Son. Jesus Christ, truly God and truly man, lived a perfect life, died on the cross bearing the punishment sinners deserved, and rose again on the third day. He is the only Mediator between God and men.

> But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.
> <cite>Romans 5:8</cite>

## Repent and believe

God commands all people everywhere to repent and believe in Christ. Salvation is by grace, through faith, not by works. Whoever trusts in Christ is forgiven, declared righteous before God and received as a child.

> For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.
> <cite>Ephesians 2:8–9</cite>
