Nuevo Pacto Reformed Baptist Church is a local congregation of baptized believers who gather every Lord's Day to worship the triune God, hear the preaching of His Word and observe the ordinances Christ gave to His Church.

## Reformed

We hold the doctrines recovered in the Protestant Reformation: Scripture alone as the rule of faith and life, salvation by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, to the glory of God alone. We confess God's sovereignty in salvation, often called "the doctrines of grace".

## Baptist

We believe baptism belongs to those who make a credible profession of faith in Christ, and that it is administered by immersion. We understand the Church as a community of regenerate believers, joined in covenant, governed by a plurality of elders and served by deacons.

## Confessional

We subscribe to the **Second London Baptist Confession of Faith of 1689** as a faithful summary of what Scripture teaches. We do not place it above the Bible; we use it as our doctrinal standard, a tool for teaching and a bond of fellowship with the churches that confess it. [Read the Confession](/en/beliefs/1689-confession/).

## Membership

Together with the Reformed and Baptist believers who went before us, we believe that every Christian should join a local church and commit to it. Our confession puts it this way:

> As all Believers are bound to joyn themselves to particular Churches, when and where they have opportunity so to do; So all that are admitted unto the priviledges of a Church, are also under the Censures and Government thereof, according to the Rule of Christ.
> <cite>1689 Confession, 26.12</cite>
